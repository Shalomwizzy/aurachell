07 — Orders & Payments

From Purchase to Delivery

The order management system tracks every sale from the moment a customer pays through to final delivery confirmation — including returns.

1
Customer Places Order
Customer fills in delivery details at checkout and proceeds to payment. Works for both registered users and guests.
2
Secure Payment via Paystack
Payment is processed securely. Card details are handled entirely by Paystack — never stored on your website.
3
Order Confirmation Emails
The customer receives a confirmation email immediately. You receive an admin notification. Stock is decremented automatically.
4
Fulfilment — Processing → Packed → Shipped → Out for Delivery
You update the order status in the admin panel as you prepare and dispatch the parcel. The customer receives an email on every status change.
5
Delivery Confirmation
When you mark an order as delivered, the customer receives a delivery confirmation email. The return window opens from this point.
6
Return Request (if applicable)
Within the admin-configured return window, the customer can submit a return request. You review it, approve or reject, and the customer is notified by email at each step.

08 — Referral Program

Turn Customers Into Brand Ambassadors

Every registered customer gets a unique referral code they can share with friends. When their friend makes a qualifying purchase, the referrer automatically earns a discount coupon — no manual action needed from you.

1
Customer Shares Their Code
Each customer has a unique code visible on their account dashboard. They share it with friends via WhatsApp, DM, or word of mouth.
2
Friend Registers Using the Code
The friend enters the code during registration. It is validated in real time — a green tick confirms the code is valid before the form is submitted. The code is also captured automatically from referral links (?ref=CODE).
3
Friend Makes a Qualifying Purchase
When the referred friend's first paid order reaches the minimum trigger amount (set by you in admin), the reward fires automatically.
4
Referrer Receives Reward Coupon by Email
The original customer receives an email with a unique discount coupon — the percentage and validity are set by you. The coupon is created instantly and can be used at checkout.

Admin Controls — Program Settings

SettingWhat it controlsDefault
Reward Discount %The percentage discount given to the referrer as a coupon10%
Trigger: Min Order AmountThe minimum amount the referred friend must spend to trigger the reward₦50,000
Coupon: Min Order to UseThe minimum basket size required to redeem the reward coupon₦5,000
Coupon ValidityNumber of days before the reward coupon expires90 days

Each customer can only be referred once. The reward only fires on the friend's very first paid order. Changing settings does not affect already-rewarded referrals.

Customers can submit a return request directly from their order history page. You review it in the admin panel, approve or reject it with notes, and the system emails the customer at every step.

Customer Experience

🔄

The shipping cost updates instantly at checkout as soon as the customer selects their delivery state — before they pay.
⚙️
Admin-Configurable
Add, edit, or remove zones and rates at any time from Admin → System → Shipping Zones. Changes apply immediately to all new orders.
📋
Shown on Invoice
The selected shipping method and fee appear on the customer's order confirmation email and downloadable invoice.

17 — Security & Fraud Protection

Multiple Layers of Protection

Several security measures run silently in the background to protect your store, your customers, and your data from abuse and automated attacks.

🍯
Honeypot Trap
A hidden field is embedded in the checkout, contact, and newsletter forms. Humans never see it, but automated bots fill it in — those submissions are silently discarded before anything is saved or processed.
⏱️
Rate Limiting
Each form has an IP-based submission limit: checkout allows 5 attempts per 10 minutes, contact and newsletter allow 3–5 per hour. Exceeding the limit returns a 429 error — the limit resets automatically after the window passes.
🔐
CSRF Protection
Every form is protected with Laravel's CSRF tokens. Requests from outside your website are automatically rejected, preventing cross-site forgery attacks.
🚫
Admin Access Control
The admin panel is completely inaccessible to regular customers. Role and permission checks enforce what each staff member can see and do.
🔒
Encrypted Passwords
All customer and staff passwords are hashed using bcrypt. Plain-text passwords are never stored anywhere in the system.
🛡️
Stock Check at Payment
Every item is validated against live stock immediately before payment is taken, preventing overselling even under simultaneous traffic.
🚧
Blocked Accounts
Any customer account can be immediately blocked from the admin panel. Blocked users are rejected at login and cannot place orders.
📄
Branded Error Pages
Custom branded error pages for 404 (not found), 500 (server error), 419 (session expired), 429 (too many requests), and 503 (maintenance). No jarring system error screens.
18 — Performance & Technical

Built for Speed, Reliability & Growth

A set of technical foundations that keep the store fast, safe, and reliable for your customers and your business.

⚡
Lazy Image Loading
All product and blog images load lazily — they only load when scrolled into view, keeping initial page load fast on slower connections.
📬
Background Email Queue
All emails are dispatched to a background queue. They never slow down your web pages, even when sending campaigns to thousands of subscribers.
💾
Automated Database Backups
Daily automated backups of your entire database run on schedule. Download any backup directly from the admin Backups page. Powered by spatie/laravel-backup.
🗺️
SEO Sitemap
A dynamic XML sitemap at /sitemap.xml covers all product, category, and blog pages. This helps Google index your store efficiently.
🤖
Robots.txt
Tells search crawlers not to index admin, account, cart, or checkout pages — only your public storefront gets indexed, keeping SEO clean.
📊
Google Analytics 4
Paste your GA4 Measurement ID in Settings → SEO and tracking goes live immediately. A live GA dashboard inside admin shows sessions, users, pageviews, and top pages.
🔗
Open Graph / Social Sharing
Every product and blog post has proper Open Graph meta tags — links shared on WhatsApp or social media show the product image, name, and description in the preview.
🗄️
Settings Cache
Store settings are cached so they don't hit the database on every page load. The cache clears automatically when you save a change.
📦
Stock Validation at Checkout
Every item is checked against live stock before payment is taken. If something sold out since it was added to the cart, the customer is informed immediately.
📱
Progressive Web App (PWA)
Customers can install Aurachell to their phone home screen like a real app. The site works offline, loads instantly on repeat visits, and supports push notifications via OneSignal.
🔔
Web Push Notifications
Integrated with OneSignal. Customers subscribe with one tap and receive branded push notifications on their device — sent directly from the OneSignal dashboard.
